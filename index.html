LIVING RESPONSIBLY IN THE MOUNTAINS

Preparation today.
Protection tomorrow.

Wildfire awareness is an important part of mountain living. Taking steps to maintain your property, plan evacuation routes, and follow official emergency alerts can help you prepare for changing conditions.

  • 01 Maintain defensible space around your home.
  • 02 Keep evacuation routes and access areas clear.
  • 03 Prepare an emergency plan for your household.
